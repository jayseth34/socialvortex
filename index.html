Frequently asked questions

THINGS YOU MIGHT ASK

What platforms do you work on?

Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube Shorts and wherever your audience is actually spending their thumbs.

How much does a social retainer cost?

It depends on volume and platforms. We price on scope, not on a number pulled out of a hat. Tell us what you have in mind and we’ll take it from there.

Can you take over an existing account?

Of course. Not everything needs to be deleted and rebuilt. Sometimes an account just needs a fresh pair of eyes and a few very intentional changes.

Do you guarantee going viral?

Nobody honest can. What we guarantee is a plan, a rhythm and content good enough to give the algorithm a real reason to push it.

Can you start yesterday?

We checked. Unfortunately, yesterday is fully booked. Send us a message and we’ll tell you when we can realistically start.
